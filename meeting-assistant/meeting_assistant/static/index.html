Which app needs macOS permission?

Double-click launcher → Terminal (/System/Applications/Utilities/Terminal.app). Started from cmux → cmux (/Applications/cmux.app). If macOS lists capture-audio itself, allow that requesting item. After changing access, fully quit and reopen the responsible launcher; save other terminal work first.

Privacy & retention

Live audio, transcription and translation stay on this Mac. Optional post-meeting text processing sends text to your chosen gateway only after confirmation. Recording saves audio and transcript events locally until you delete them. Exports are separate copies. Review consent and your organization’s policy before recording.

Sessions and exports: ~/Library/Application Support/Meeting Assistant/sessions. Delete session folders and exports there when no longer needed; backups or other copies must be removed separately.
